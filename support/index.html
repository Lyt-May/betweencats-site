Common questions / 常见问题

Restore purchases / 恢复购买

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Connect to the internet, open the game's purchase screen, and tap Restore Purchases. Restoration recovers purchase access, not your game progress. If it still fails, contact us with the error message.

使用购买时的 Apple 账号，联网后在游戏购买界面点击“恢复购买”。恢复的是购买权益，不是游戏进度。如仍失败，请将错误提示发送给我们。

Which levels are free? / 哪些关卡免费？

The iOS edition provides the first 12 main levels for free. A one-time purchase unlocks the remaining main levels and return levels. There is no auto-renewing subscription. Normal level progression still applies.

iOS 版本的前 12 个正式关卡免费。一次性内购解锁其余正式关卡与归途关卡，无自动续费订阅；后续关卡仍按通关顺序开放。

Save an image or video / 保存图片或视频

Saving to Photos requires permission to add photos. Check the app's Photos permission in iOS Settings and allow adding photos, then return to the game and try again. Playing the game does not require this permission.

保存到相册需要添加照片权限。请在 iOS 系统设置中检查本应用的照片权限，允许添加照片后返回重试。正常游戏不需要此权限。

Game progress / 游戏进度

Progress is stored on your device. Restore Purchases does not transfer progress between devices. Uninstalling the app may remove local progress.

进度保存在设备上。“恢复购买”不会在设备间迁移进度。卸载应用可能删除本机进度。

Refunds / 退款

Apple processes in-app purchases and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page to request a refund. If Apple revokes purchase access, paid levels become unavailable; game progress is retained in the app.

内购付款和退款申请由 Apple 处理，可访问 Apple 问题报告页面申请退款。购买权益撤销后付费关卡将受限，应用内已有进度保留。
