English

This policy describes the current iOS app, this website, and support email for BetweenCats, a game by Maymaybe.

Game data

Game progress, settings, and local game records are stored on your device. The current iOS app has no account registration, advertising, or developer-operated analytics. We do not upload these local records to a developer server or use them for advertising tracking.

In-app purchases

Apple processes payments through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to verify access to purchased content. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple handles its information under its own privacy policy.

Photos and sharing

The app requests permission to add content to your photo library only when you choose to save a gameplay image or video. You can decline this permission and continue playing. Sharing may place your selected content on the clipboard or pass it to the system share interface or another app you choose. External apps and services handle submitted content under their own policies.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate reported issues. Messages are kept as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request deletion; any information we must retain will be explained. Gmail processes support email under Google’s privacy policy.

This website

This static website does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if visitors are not signed in. GitHub handles hosting data under its privacy statement.

Deletion and your choices

Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data from the device. This does not remove saved Photos, clipboard content, material shared to third parties, or Apple’s purchase records. You can manage Photos permission in iOS Settings and contact us about support email or privacy questions.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date. The game’s developer uses the name Maymaybe. For privacy questions, contact maymaybe513@gmail.com.

Third-party policies: Apple, GitHub, Google.

简体中文

本政策说明由 Maymaybe 开发的鼠有余地（BetweenCats）当前 iOS 应用、本网站及客服邮件涉及的信息处理。

游戏数据

游戏进度、设置和本地游戏记录保存在设备上。当前 iOS 应用不提供账号注册、广告或开发者运营的分析服务。我们不会将这些本地记录上传到开发者服务器，也不将其用于广告跟踪。

应用内购买

Apple 通过 App Store 处理付款。应用使用 StoreKit 验证已购内容的访问权益。我们不会收到您的支付卡信息。Apple 按其隐私政策处理相关信息。

照片与分享

仅当您选择保存游戏图片或视频时，应用才请求向照片图库添加内容的权限。您可以拒绝并继续游戏。分享功能可能将您选择的内容放入剪贴板，或交给系统分享界面及您选择的其他应用。外部应用与服务按各自政策处理您提交的内容。

客服邮件

当您发送邮件时，我们会收到您的邮箱地址、邮件内容及您主动发送的附件，并用于回复请求和排查问题。邮件按支持需要和适用法律义务保留。您可以联系我们请求删除；如有必须保留的信息，我们会说明。Gmail 按 Google 隐私政策处理客服邮件。

本网站

本静态网站未添加访问分析、广告跟踪或联系表单。网站托管在 GitHub Pages。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访客 IP 地址，即使访客未登录。GitHub 按其隐私声明处理托管数据。

删除与您的选择

卸载应用通常会移除设备上的应用本地数据，但不会删除已保存到照片图库、复制到剪贴板、分享至第三方的内容或 Apple 的购买记录。您可以在 iOS 设置中管理照片权限，并联系我们处理客服邮件或隐私问题。

更新与联系

本政策的更新会在此页面公布并更新日期。游戏开发者使用 Maymaybe 署名。隐私问题请联系 maymaybe513@gmail.com。

第三方政策：Apple、GitHub、Google。
